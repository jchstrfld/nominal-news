Our Editorial Approach

We aim for neutral, factual summaries of selected reporting. We group related articles, select representative headlines and descriptions, and instruct an AI model to use only supplied facts, avoid unsupported interpretations, and acknowledge uncertainty. Linked sources show broader coverage, with repeat articles grouped together. Political ratings describe the outlets, not our summaries, and do not determine what we write.
